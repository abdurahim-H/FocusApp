Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cosmic Focus (the "Service", accessible at universefocuses.com) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data stored locally on your device

Most of what Cosmic Focus stores is kept in your own browser, on your own device, using localStorage and sessionStorage. This includes:

  • Your tasks, focus session history, and statistics
  • Your settings (timer durations, scene quality, keyboard shortcuts, sound preferences, etc.)
  • Saved focus presets and schedules
  • UI state such as the position of the mini timer

This data never leaves your device unless you are signed into an account (see below). Clearing your browser storage for this site removes it permanently.

2. Data stored in your account (optional)

If you choose to create an account, we use Supabase to store a copy of your productivity data (tasks, stats, settings, presets) so it can follow you across devices. The account stores:

  • Your email address (and, if you sign in with a third-party provider, the identifier they return)
  • The productivity data listed above, keyed to your account
  • Timestamps of account creation, last sign-in, and last sync

You can use Cosmic Focus anonymously without ever creating an account. You can export or delete all account data at any time from Settings → Data & About.

3. Third-party services

The Service loads resources from the following third parties when you use it:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and provides security/CDN services.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter font family.
  • Babylon.js CDN — serves the 3D rendering library.
  • Supabase — powers optional accounts and cloud sync.
  • Stripe — processes Pro tier subscription payments. Stripe receives only what's needed to bill you (card details, billing address). We never see your card number.
  • Spotify — only when you explicitly connect your Spotify account from the Streaming tab in the Music drawer (Pro feature). See section 4 for what we receive and store.

These providers may receive your IP address and basic request metadata as part of delivering their service. We do not share additional information with them beyond what each integration requires.

4. Music-service integrations (optional)

If you choose to connect a music streaming service from Music → Streaming, we use the standard OAuth handshake provided by that service. Today this covers Spotify; YouTube Music, Apple Music, YouTube, and SoundCloud are scaffolded but not yet wired.

For Spotify specifically, when you click Connect we request the following scopes and use them as follows:

  • user-read-email + user-read-private — to display your Spotify name + Premium status in the connected-account chip and tooltip.
  • streaming + user-modify-playback-state + user-read-playback-state + user-read-currently-playing — to render the "Now playing" mini-player and let the play / pause / next / previous buttons control your active Spotify device.

What we receive from Spotify and where it lives:

  • Display name + email + Premium status — stored in your browser's localStorage under the key fu_spotify_user. Used only for the UI label and to gate the Premium-only playback controls. Never sent to our servers.
  • Access token + refresh token — stored in your browser's localStorage under the key fu_spotify_tokens. Used to call Spotify's API on your behalf when you interact with the mini-player or search panel. Never sent to our servers.
  • Currently-playing track metadata — fetched live from Spotify when the mini-player is on screen. Not stored.

We don't have a server-side database of your music data. Everything related to the Spotify integration lives only in your own browser. To revoke at any time:

  • From Cosmic Focus — right-click the Spotify chip in Music → Streaming and confirm Disconnect. This clears the tokens and profile from your local storage.
  • From Spotify — visit your Spotify Apps page, find "Cosmic Focus", and click Remove Access. This invalidates the tokens server-side.

Cosmic Focus is not affiliated with Spotify AB. Any audio content you listen to is delivered by Spotify under their own terms and licences.

5. Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track individual users across sites. It provides aggregate information about page views and performance. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

6. Cookies

Cosmic Focus does not set tracking cookies. Supabase may set a session cookie to keep you signed in if you have an account. That cookie is used only for authentication.

7. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Export your data as JSON or CSV from Settings → Data & About.
  • Delete everything stored locally via Settings → Data & About → Clear all data.
  • Delete your account and all associated cloud data from Settings → Account.
  • Disconnect any music streaming service from Music → Streaming by right-clicking its chip.

Under the GDPR and CCPA you also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us as described below.

8. Children

Cosmic Focus is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
