2. Categories of personal data we process

2.1 Local-only data (your browser)

Most of what Cosmic Focus stores never leaves your device. The following lives in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage (these are HTML5 storage technologies, distinct from cookies):

  • Your tasks, focus session history, and statistics
  • Your settings (timer durations, scene quality, keyboard shortcuts, sound preferences, scene theme)
  • Saved focus presets and schedules
  • Position of the mini-timer + Spotify mini-player widgets
  • If you connect Spotify: an access + refresh token (key fu_spotify_tokens) and your Spotify profile (key fu_spotify_user) — see section 4

This data is not transmitted to us and we have no access to it.

2.2 Account data (only if you sign in)

Sign-in is optional. Anonymous use is fully featured. If you create an account, we process:

  • Email address
  • Hashed password (when using email/password) or third-party identifier (Google OAuth, magic link)
  • Display name and unique handle (username)
  • Account creation, last sign-in, and last sync timestamps
  • Focus-session log records (table public.sessions)

The Privacy Policy previously stated that tasks / settings / presets are mirrored to the cloud. That is the planned next phase but is not yet implemented. Today, only sessions / billing / account-identity tables exist server-side.

2.3 Subscription data (only if you upgrade to Pro)

If you purchase a Pro subscription, we process:

  • Your Stripe customer ID and subscription ID
  • Subscription status (active, trialing, past_due, canceled) and the period-end timestamp
  • Pro tier flag ('pro' / 'free') in the public.billing table

Payment card data is processed entirely by Stripe and never reaches us.

4. Music-service integrations (optional, consent-based)

If you click Connect on a service in Music → Streaming, you authorize us to call that service's API on your behalf. This is processed under your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of past processing.

Today this is wired only for Spotify. The scopes we request are:

  • user-read-email + user-read-private — to display your Spotify name and detect Premium status
  • streaming + user-modify-playback-state + user-read-playback-state + user-read-currently-playing — to render the "Now playing" widget and let the play / pause / next / previous buttons control your active Spotify device

We receive your Spotify display name, email, and product tier (free / premium / open). Tokens and profile live in your browser's localStorage only — they are never sent to our servers. To revoke:

  • From Cosmic Focus — right-click the Spotify chip in Music → Streaming and confirm Disconnect.
  • From Spotify — open spotify.com/account/apps and remove access for "Cosmic Focus".

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures (Art. 32 GDPR), including:

  • HTTPS / TLS for all traffic, with HSTS
  • Strict Content-Security-Policy without 'unsafe-inline'
  • Hashed + salted passwords (bcrypt, managed by Supabase Auth)
  • Have-I-Been-Pwned breach screening at sign-up (k-anonymity — only the first 5 SHA-1 chars leave your browser)
  • Postgres Row-Level Security restricting per-user data access
  • PKCE for all OAuth flows
  • Stripe webhook signatures verified before persisting billing state

10. Cookies and similar storage technologies

Cosmic Focus does not set tracking cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless and does not track individual users across sites.

Supabase Auth, when you sign in, stores your session token using localStorage (browser storage, not a cookie). For implementation simplicity, this is the standard Supabase JS SDK behavior.

Stripe sets a small number of strictly-necessary cookies on its own checkout / portal pages (which run on stripe.com, not on universefocuses.com) for fraud detection and session management. Those cookies are subject to Stripe's own privacy policy.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process personal data of children. If you are under 16 and reside in the EU, please do not create an account or click Connect on any music service. If we become aware that we hold data of a child under 16, we will delete it.

12. Microphone access

The Pro Notes feature includes optional voice dictation, which uses your browser's built-in SpeechRecognition API. The Permissions Policy declares microphone=(self) so the browser may prompt for access when you click the dictation button. Audio is processed by your browser / OS speech-recognition service and not stored or transmitted by Cosmic Focus.
