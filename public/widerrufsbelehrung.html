Right of Withdrawal · Widerrufsbelehrung

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This page provides the statutory right-of-withdrawal information for consumers in the European Union, in accordance with Article 246a of the EGBGB / §312g of the German Civil Code (BGB) and Directive 2011/83/EU on consumer rights.

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period will expire 14 days from the day of the conclusion of the contract.

To exercise the right of withdrawal, you must inform us:

  • Name: Abdurahim Hudulov
  • Address: Silcherstraße 72, 74074 Heilbronn, Deutschland
  • Email: abduh@universefocuses.com

of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal statement (e.g. a letter sent by post or email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but it is not obligatory.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication concerning your exercise of the right of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you, including the costs of delivery (with the exception of the supplementary costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive type of standard delivery offered by us), without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.

Special provisions for digital services (§356(5) BGB)

Important. The right of withdrawal expires prematurely for a digital service (such as a Cosmic Focus Pro subscription) if we have begun the performance of the contract after you have given your express prior consent that performance start before the end of the withdrawal period and have acknowledged that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal as soon as performance has commenced.

When you check out for a Pro subscription, Stripe Checkout includes a tickbox in which you give exactly this consent and acknowledgement. This is a statutory requirement; without your consent, your Pro subscription cannot start.

If you complete checkout, performance begins immediately (your Pro features become active). At that point, your right of withdrawal under §312g BGB is extinguished by operation of §356(5) BGB. You can still cancel your subscription at any time under section 5.4 of our Terms of Service — that is a contractual cancellation, not a statutory withdrawal.

Outside the 14-day withdrawal period (and the digital-content exception above), our cancellation and refund terms are described in section 5 of our Terms of Service. In particular:

  • You may cancel a Pro subscription at any time, free of charge, from Settings → Account → Manage subscription (Stripe Customer Portal) or by emailing abduh@universefocuses.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; Pro features remain available until then.
